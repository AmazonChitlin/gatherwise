# Gatherwise recruiter walkthrough

## Best route

1. Start on `/showcase`
2. Click `Try the readiness demo`
3. Open a fictional demo scenario from the intake page
4. Land on results and show:
   - readiness summary
   - Evidence Trail
   - official source links
   - Readiness Route
   - Change Simulator

## What to emphasize

- The product solves a real research and trust problem.
- AI has a narrow, explicit role.
- Deterministic rules and official sources are the authority.
- Unsupported jurisdictions are refused instead of guessed.
- The product includes evaluation and reliability work, not just interface polish.

## What not to claim

- Do not claim nationwide support.
- Do not claim legal determination.
- Do not claim that measured usability outcomes already exist if they have not been collected.
- Do not claim live-model extraction accuracy from the offline fixture harness.
