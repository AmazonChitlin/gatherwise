# Gatherwise Design Principle Audit

Date: 2026-07-13
Status: principle board for concept review

## Emphasis

- Use: one dominant CTA and one dominant route explanation per screen
- Screen/component connection: concept hero and requirement card
- Why it matters: supports message clarity and prevents the interface from feeling like a dashboard collage

## Contrast

- Use: strong contrast between primary path, evidence path, and uncertainty state
- Screen/component connection: status marker, error state, evidence tab
- Why it matters: helps users distinguish verified, unresolved, and actionable information quickly

## Balance

- Use: pair expressive route visuals with disciplined conventional controls
- Screen/component connection: hero plus intake preview
- Why it matters: keeps the interface distinctive without making basic actions unfamiliar

## Alignment

- Use: align route stops, evidence cards, and fact chips to a shared structural rhythm
- Screen/component connection: Route Ledger concept and progress strip
- Why it matters: reinforces causal reading order and improves recruiter comprehension of architecture

## Repetition

- Use: repeat the four signature motifs consistently across views
- Screen/component connection: route line, evidence tab, status marker, local grid
- Why it matters: creates recognizable product identity without inventing new shapes per screen

## Flow

- Use: create a clear read order from event facts to route stop to evidence anchor
- Screen/component connection: requirement card and side evidence panel
- Why it matters: supports Outcome 3 and Hypothesis H7

## Proximity

- Use: place the cause, state, and source near each other
- Screen/component connection: requirement card metadata and source card
- Why it matters: reduces the chance that users see a requirement but miss why it appeared

## White Space

- Use: preserve breathing room around explanation-heavy components
- Screen/component connection: heroes, route cards, mobile view
- Why it matters: prevents the “dense municipal portal” feeling and helps first-time users stay oriented

## Visual Tension

- Use: combine measured grid structure with slightly tactile evidence and route treatments
- Screen/component connection: Field Guide evidence tab and Local Signal markers
- Why it matters: gives the product character without losing readability

## Progressive Disclosure

- Use: surface the next most important route step first, then reveal deeper evidence or comparison layers
- Screen/component connection: tabs, evidence cards, future route drill-downs
- Why it matters: supports novices while leaving room for advanced users and recruiter review

## Principle Summary

- Distinctive style should come from meaningful structure.
- Wayfinding and provenance should do more work than decoration.
- The product should feel guided, not theatrical.
