## What To Look For

### Message Clarity

- After 10 seconds, can you explain what the product does?
- Does the hero communicate event-readiness guidance rather than generic AI help or permit filing?
- Is there only one dominant CTA?

### Trust And Evidence

- Can you tell the difference between:
  - user/input fact
  - requirement/result
  - official source
  - missing or unresolved fact
- Does the evidence tab feel useful instead of decorative?

### Route Comprehension

- Can you explain why the sample requirement appeared?
- Do route stops and progress states feel meaningful?
- Would this structure support future comparison views?

### Familiar Interaction Anchors

- Do nav, buttons, forms, tabs, breadcrumbs, and focus states still feel standard?
- Does the concept stay visually distinctive without becoming behaviorally novel?

### Mobile Readability

- Does the mobile view stay readable and purposeful?
- Are the status marker and evidence cues still clear on a small screen?

### Recruiter Readability

- Could this direction support a quick architecture explanation?
- Does it make the AI boundary and evidence chain feel presentable?
