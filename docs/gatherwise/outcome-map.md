# Gatherwise Outcome Map

Date: 2026-07-13
Status: measurable planning outcomes, not feature commitments

## Primary User Outcomes

### Outcome 1

A first-time organizer reaches a useful, source-backed summary without understanding municipal terminology.

- Observable evidence:
  - User can state what to check next in plain language.
  - User can find at least one official source tied to the result.
  - User does not need facilitator translation of government terms.

### Outcome 2

A user can identify which missing event details may change the result.

- Observable evidence:
  - User can point to at least one unresolved fact that may change outcome or confidence.
  - User understands whether the gap is about venue, food, alcohol, traffic, attendance, or jurisdiction.

### Outcome 3

A user can explain why a possible requirement appeared.

- Observable evidence:
  - User can connect the requirement to a fact pattern, rule logic, or jurisdiction.
  - User distinguishes “appeared because of your facts” from “appeared because this is a global/safety check.”

### Outcome 4

A recruiter can understand the hybrid AI architecture within 90 seconds.

- Observable evidence:
  - Recruiter can explain the role of AI, deterministic rule logic, and official-source evidence.
  - Recruiter can identify at least one area showing Paul’s product or engineering contribution.

### Outcome 5

The product never presents unsupported jurisdictions or missing evidence as verified guidance.

- Observable evidence:
  - Users can tell when a jurisdiction is unsupported or partially supported.
  - Unsupported or low-evidence states do not visually mimic verified guidance.

### Outcome 6

The manual path remains usable when AI is unavailable.

- Observable evidence:
  - A participant can complete a manual flow and still receive useful structured guidance.
  - Loss of AI does not collapse the entire experience or remove clarity about next steps.

## Secondary Outcomes

- Users feel less overwhelmed within the first 90 seconds.
- Experienced organizers can spot what changed across jurisdictions or event facts faster than with manual website comparison alone.
- Vendors and food operators can separate their responsibilities from organizer responsibilities more confidently.
- Recruiters leave with a clearer sense of system boundaries, trust posture, and implementation quality.

## Anti-Outcomes To Avoid

- Users think Gatherwise guarantees compliance.
- Users assume unsupported jurisdictions are covered because the interface looks polished.
- Users cannot tell what came from them versus what came from AI or rules.
- Recruiters assume the system is “just AI” because the deterministic and evidence layers are invisible.

## Suggested Success Signals

- Message clarity success in first-glance tests
- Faster task completion to source-backed summary
- Higher explanation accuracy for “why this appeared”
- Lower confusion between evidence types
- Correct recruiter explanation of architecture and AI boundary
